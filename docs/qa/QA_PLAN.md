**What is already solid** (verified in code and by the test runs, and not re-planned):

- Tenant RLS fails closed, is FORCE'd on 222/240 relations, and is audited in CI.
- JWTs are RS256 with alg/kid/iss/aud pinned; the JTI denylist fails closed; refresh tokens rotate with reuse detection.
- Argon2id passwords use constant-time branches; setup, invitation and verification tokens carry 80–256 bits.
- Stripe, Tap and Apple webhooks are signature-verified, idempotent, and folded in order.
- ClamAV scanning fails closed on the generic upload path.
- AES-256-GCM sealing uses fresh nonces and context AAD.
- No SSRF sinks, no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, no third-party analytics.
- The mobile app keeps tokens in secure storage, uses PKCE in a system browser, and binds the redirect to an intent.
- Test baseline: 4,187 API tests, 798 security tests, 558 workers tests, 168 db tests and 1,208 Flutter tests pass, with 0 failures.

The gaps are concentrated in **routes that skipped the permission layer**, **identity lifecycle** (suspension, password change, role change), **the production DB login and operating posture** (backups, alerts, deploy gate), and **children's-data compliance**.

### Top 10 risks

1. **QA-007** [CRITICAL][SEC] Public school self-registration returns its verification and invitation tokens, so anyone can activate an unreviewed school impersonating a real one: Anyone can stand up an **active school impersonating a real one** with no review, because the public registration API returns its own email-verification and admin-invitation tokens. Minors and parents could then be invited under that name.
2. **QA-014** [HIGH][SEC] Runtime DB login is Supabase `postgres` (probably BYPASSRLS), and some code depends on bypassing RLS: RLS is **not a real backstop** in production: every service logs in as Supabase `postgres` (very probably BYPASSRLS), and some code already depends on bypass. One SQL bug could then read every school's minors' data.
3. **QA-008** [HIGH][SEC] Students and parents can create, edit, delete and AI-expose class learning materials (no permission or ownership check): **Students and parents can delete, deface, or push to the US LLM** any learning material in their class: the routes have no permission or ownership check.
4. **QA-010** [HIGH][SEC] Deactivated or suspended users can sign back in via Google/Microsoft and keep refreshing; PATCH status revokes nothing: **Deactivated staff sign straight back in** with Google/Microsoft and keep their old roles; PATCH-suspended users keep refreshing forever.
5. **QA-019** [HIGH][SEC] The production branch (`dev`) logs raw query strings, including the Google Play RTDN secret, to Axiom: The production branch logs **raw query strings to Axiom**, including the Google Play RTDN secret. The fix exists only on `stage`.
6. **QA-021** [HIGH][SEC] `.dockerignore` doesn't exclude `prod-secrets*.json` or `infra/terraform`; production secrets sit in the build context: **Production secret files and Terraform state** are in the Docker build context. A documented laptop deploy uploads them to Fly's remote builder.
7. **QA-022** [HIGH][BUG] ERPNext Sales Invoice POST is retried with no idempotency, so parents can be billed twice: **Parents can be invoiced twice**: the ERPNext invoice POST is auto-retried without idempotency, made worse by SIGKILL on every deploy (QA-047).
8. **QA-012** [HIGH][SEC] AI add-on and legacy school checkout accept any active price, so access can be bought at the wrong, cheaper price: **AI add-on (and legacy school plan) can be bought at any other plan's price**, because the checkout doesn't bind the price to its plan.
9. **QA-045** [HIGH][INFRA] No backups, PITR, RPO/RTO or tested restore for Supabase, R2 or Redis: **No proven backups or restore** (Supabase PITR, R2 versioning, drill), and **no alerting** at all (QA-046): data loss or a silent outage would go unnoticed and unrecoverable.
10. **QA-076** [HIGH][PRIVACY] GDPR Art. 8: children consent and pay for the AI add-on themselves, with no parental consent: **GDPR Art. 8**: children consent to and pay for an AI add-on that sends their prompts to a US provider, with no parental consent. The compliance dossier contradicts the Irish privacy policy (QA-077).
