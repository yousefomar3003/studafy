This agreement is between **Studafy Limited** ("Studafy", "we", "us"), a company registered in Ireland under number 822430, of 4/5 The Square, Cahir, Co. Tipperary, Ireland, and the **school** named in the application to use Studafy (the "School").

The person who submits the School's application accepts this agreement for the School and confirms that they are authorised to do so. The agreement takes effect when we approve the application.

It has two parts. **Part A** covers the service. **Part B** contains the data processing terms that apply when we handle personal data for the School, as Article 28 of the GDPR requires. The annexes form part of Part B. The [Terms of Service](/terms) apply to each person who uses Studafy, and the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains how personal data is handled.

## Part A: The service

### 1. Definitions

- **Service**: Studafy, the school management system we provide on the web (app.studafy.com) and in the Studafy mobile apps, with its supporting systems.
- **Users**: the people the School gives access to the Service, such as administrators, school leaders, teachers, other staff, students, and parents or guardians.
- **School Data**: the information the School and its Users put into the Service, or that the Service creates for the School, including personal data.
- **Data Protection Law**: the laws on personal data that apply to the processing: the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988–2018, the Jordanian Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023, and any other data protection law that applies to the School.
- **controller**, **processor**, **personal data**, **processing**, **data subject** and **personal data breach** have the meanings given in the GDPR.

### 2. The service

2.1 We provide the Service to the School for its own educational and administrative purposes, under this agreement.

2.2 The Service changes over time. We may add, change or remove features. We will not make a change that substantially reduces the core functions the School relies on (student records, attendance, timetables, homework and marks, communication with families, and fees) without telling the School's administrators at least 30 days in advance, unless the law, security or a third party's change requires us to act sooner.

2.3 Some parts of the Service depend on services the School or its Users choose, such as signing in with Google or Microsoft and downloading the apps from Google Play or the App Store. Those services are provided under their own terms.

### 3. Fees

3.1 The Service is free for schools. Every school has a free plan, which does not expire, and we do not charge the School for it.

3.2 The School may choose to buy paid licences, which give extra features to the Users it assigns a seat to, such as students, teachers or parents. A licence applies only under a written quote from us that the School accepts. The quote sets out the features, the number of seats, the period, the price, the currency and any taxes. When the School accepts a quote, we issue an invoice, which the School pays by bank transfer to the account, with the payment reference and within the time shown on the invoice. The licence starts when we receive payment, or earlier if we agree. Only Users assigned a seat are covered. If a quote and this agreement differ on a licence's features, seats, period, price or payment, the quote applies.

3.3 If an invoice is still unpaid after its due date and after we have reminded the School, we may suspend the paid features it covers until it is paid. The free Service continues.

3.4 We will not start charging for any part of the Service that is currently free without telling the School's administrators at least 90 days in advance. Such fees apply to the School only if it agrees to them in writing. If it does not, it may end this agreement before the fees start, under clause 10.

3.5 Students may buy the optional AI Study Assistant for themselves in the Studafy mobile app, through Google Play or the App Store. That is a separate consumer contract between the student (or their parent or guardian) and us, on the [Terms of Service](/terms). The School is never charged for it. If the School later gives a student AI access through a licence, we stop the student's own renewal where the store allows it.

### 4. The School's responsibilities

4.1 The School decides who receives an account, what role each User has, and what School Data is entered. It removes access for people who leave.

4.2 The School makes sure that:

- it has a lawful basis under Data Protection Law for the School Data it puts into the Service, and for the instructions it gives us;
- the people whose data is in the Service are told how it is used, in the School's own privacy notice, which may refer to our [Privacy Policy](/privacy);
- where the law requires consent, including a parent's or guardian's consent for a child, it obtains and records that consent;
- the School Data it enters is accurate and limited to what it needs;
- its administrators keep their sign-in secure and give each User only the access their role needs;
- where the law requires consent for its students' use of the AI study features, including a parent's or guardian's consent for a child, it obtains and records that consent;
- its Users follow the [Terms of Service](/terms).

4.3 The Service has no built-in field for government identification. The School may record government identification numbers or documents only where the law requires or allows it to, only for that purpose, and only in fields of its own record templates that it marks as sensitive (clause 4.5). It must not use the Service to match or link people by such identifiers. The Service itself never uses record data, or any email address, phone number or name, to find, match or link people.

4.4 The School is responsible for what it does with School Data outside the Service, including anything it downloads, exports or prints.

4.5 The School can create its own record templates (for students, parents, staff and classes) and its own lists. It decides which fields they contain and collects only what it needs. It must mark as sensitive any field that holds health information, government identification or other special category or sensitive data. Values in sensitive fields are encrypted, shown masked, and can be revealed only by the School's administrators and principal (for staff records, by administrators only), and every reveal is recorded in the audit log. Sensitive values in record templates are deleted automatically at the end of the retention period the School sets for the field, which is 12 months after the person leaves the School unless the School chooses otherwise. Sensitive columns in the School's own lists are not deleted automatically; the School deletes them when it no longer needs them.

4.6 Users can report messages, and content produced by the AI study features, from inside the Service. The Service sends these reports to the School's administrators (and, for messages, its principal), who review them and act under the School's own safeguarding procedures. Reports cannot be deleted, because they form part of the School's safeguarding record. We do not review reports as a matter of course, but we may act, including by reporting to the authorities, where the law requires it or where a child is at serious risk and the School cannot act.

- error reports, crash reports and application logs, which we use to keep the Service working;

- the AI Study Assistant a student buys for themselves, including its purchase and billing records;
- quotes, invoices and payments for the School's licences;

## Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

### Separation of schools and access control

- Every record belongs to one school. The database applies row-level security policies so that, in a school's context, only that school's data can be read or changed, and automated tests check that one school cannot reach another's data.
- Access is role-based. Each role sees only what it needs: for example, teachers see the students and classes they teach, parents see only their own children's released records, and attendance staff do not see marks, discipline or evaluations.
- Messages between a parent and a teacher can be read only by those two people. Message text is not copied into the audit log.
- Studafy platform roles exist only in our own platform account and cannot be given to anyone at a school (clause B3.2).

### Sign-in and sessions

- Sign-in with Google or Microsoft, or with a member ID and password. School leaders, administrators and finance staff must use Google or Microsoft.
- Passwords are stored only as argon2id hashes. Setup codes, invitation tokens and session tokens are stored only as hashes.
- After five wrong passwords, a member ID account is locked for 15 minutes.
- Access tokens expire after 15 minutes. Sessions can be revoked, and all of a User's sessions end when their account is deleted or the School's account is closed.

### Encryption

- All connections to the Service use encrypted HTTPS (TLS). Connections from our servers to the database use TLS with full certificate verification.
- Data is encrypted at rest by our database and file storage providers.
- Values in fields the School marks as sensitive are additionally encrypted by us with AES-256-GCM before they are stored, with keys held as production secrets. They are shown masked, can be revealed only by authorised roles, and each reveal is recorded in the audit log before the value is decrypted. Sensitive documents are downloaded through links that expire after one minute.

### Monitoring and logging

- An append-only audit log records the actions of school staff and our platform staff, including reveals of sensitive fields, downloads of sensitive documents, and searches.
- Security events, such as blocked cross-site requests, sign-in attempts blocked by rate limits, and reuse of revoked session tokens, are logged.
- Errors from the servers, web app and mobile apps are reported to an error monitoring service, with personal data removed or reduced to a user identifier on the server side.

### Protecting the Service

- Rate limits on sign-in, messaging, AI and other requests.
- Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile) on public forms, including the contact form, school registration and the public account deletion page.
- Study materials are scanned for viruses before they are made available. Infected files are quarantined, and the uploader is told.
- HTML is removed from text that Users write, such as announcements, comments and submissions.
- Files are downloaded through links that expire after a few minutes.

### AI study features

- Students must agree to the data sharing before using the AI study features, and can withdraw.
- No user identifier, name, email address, marks or attendance is sent to the AI provider.
- Questions that appear to contain phone numbers, email addresses, card numbers or identity numbers are blocked.
- Photos are converted to text on our own servers and are not sent to the AI provider.
- Conversations are deleted after 90 days.

### Hosting, resilience and backups

- Application servers, the database, job queues and logs are hosted in the European Union, in Frankfurt, Germany.
- The database is backed up by our database provider as a managed service. Backups expire in the normal course under the provider's backup cycle.

### Organisation

- Staff who process School Data are bound by confidentiality and have access only as far as their work requires.
- Production secrets are held in one restricted store and supplied to the Service at deployment.
- The Service carries no advertising and no analytics or advertising tracking software.
