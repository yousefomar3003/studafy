# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

This policy explains how personal data is handled in Studafy, the school management system made by Studafy Limited: the web app at app.studafy.com, the Studafy mobile apps, our website, and when people contact us. It is for everyone who uses Studafy: school leaders and staff, teachers, parents and guardians, and students.

Most of what is in Studafy is a school's records, and for those your school decides what is collected and why; we handle them for the school. A smaller set of data we handle for our own purposes, and for that we are responsible. Section 2 explains the difference, and each part of this policy says which applies.

## Summary

- **Your school creates your account.** Nobody signs up to Studafy alone. The school creates accounts for its staff, students and parents, decides what it records about them, and links parents and guardians to their children.
- **For school records, your school is in charge.** The school is the "controller" of its records, and we are its "processor": we handle them only on the school's instructions, under our [School Agreement](/school-agreement). For questions or requests about them, contact your school first.
- **For a few things, we are in charge:** sign-in and security (including IP addresses), running and fixing the service, school applications and sales inquiries, the AI study assistant when a student buys it for themselves, our communications with school administrators, our website, and our own legal obligations.
- **We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or use analytics or advertising trackers.** We do not use school records to train AI models.
- **The AI study assistant is optional.** It sends a student's questions and the text of class materials to Together AI in the United States. Each student agrees for themselves first and can withdraw at any time. Students under 16 should use or buy it only with a parent's or guardian's permission. Conversations are kept for 90 days.
- **Notifications can contain personal details,** such as names, marks, assignment titles, fee amounts and short message previews.
- **Our main systems are in the EU** (Frankfurt, Germany). Some providers process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards in section 9.
- **You can delete your account** in the app or at [/legal/delete-account](/legal/delete-account). You have 14 days to change your mind; erasure is then completed within 30 days. Some records stay, because they are the school's records or the law requires them (section 11).
- **You have rights** to access, correct and delete your data, and more, and can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission or your local authority. Section 13 covers people in Jordan.

## 2. Who is responsible for your data

The "controller" decides why and how personal data is used and carries the main responsibility for it. A "processor" handles personal data for a controller, on its instructions.

### 2.1 Your school, for School Data

For School Data, **your school is the controller and we are its processor.** This covers almost everything in Studafy: profiles and records of students, parents and staff, classes and timetables, attendance, assignments, submitted work, marks, results, certificates and reports, discipline records, teacher evaluations, messages between parents and teachers, announcements and notifications about school matters, fees, and files the school and its users upload.

Your school decides what to record, who can see it, how long to keep it and which features to use. It is responsible for having a lawful basis, for telling you about it in its own privacy notice, and for obtaining any consent the law requires. We process School Data only to provide Studafy to the school, under the [School Agreement](/school-agreement), which contains the data processing terms required by Article 28 of the GDPR.

**For questions or requests about School Data, please contact your school first.** If you write to us about School Data, we pass your request to your school without undue delay, tell you we have done so, and help the school to respond.

### 2.2 Studafy, for our own purposes

We are the controller for personal data we use for our own purposes:

- sign-in and account security: sessions, sign-in records, security events, audit and rate-limit records, including IP addresses;
- operating, securing and improving the service: logs, error reports and mobile crash reports;
- applications from schools to use Studafy, and sales inquiries;
- the AI study assistant when a student buys it for themselves: the purchase, the entitlement and the AI processing for that add-on (section 5);
- licence quotes, invoices and payments between Studafy and schools, and our communications with school administrators;
- our public website;
- our own legal obligations, and establishing or defending legal claims.

For these, contact us at [hello@studafy.com](mailto:hello@studafy.com).

### 2.3 Independent controllers

Some companies act as controllers in their own right, under their own privacy notices: Google and Microsoft when you sign in with them; Apple and Google when you download our apps or buy through their stores; and a payment provider when you pay a school fee online (section 8).

## 3. The personal data we handle

### 3.1 Your account and sign-in

**Responsible:** your school for your account as part of School Data; Studafy for sign-in security.

Your school creates your account and chooses your role (for example administrator, principal, finance, teacher, attendance officer, parent or student). For each account we hold your name as the school entered it, your email address if you have one, your role and school, a member code Studafy assigns (such as one starting `STU-` for a student), your account status and last sign-in, your language, time zone and notification quiet hours, invitations sent to you and by whom, and when you accepted the Terms of Service and confirmed reading this policy, with the versions.

**Signing in.** You sign in with Google or Microsoft, or with a member ID and password.

- With Google or Microsoft, we receive an identifier for your account, your email address and basic profile information. We keep only the provider's name and the identifier, and use your email address to find the account your school created. We never receive your Google or Microsoft password.
- Students, parents, teachers and attendance officers can use a member ID and password instead; school leaders and finance staff must use Google or Microsoft. Your school gives you a one-time setup code, valid for 14 days, to set your password. We store passwords only in hashed form, so nobody at Studafy or your school can read them. After five failed attempts, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes.

**Sessions and devices.** For each sign-in we record the device name, the browser or app details (the "user agent") and your IP address. If you allow notifications in the mobile app, we register your device's push token and platform. **Your school's administrators can see the sessions and devices of people in their school, including IP addresses.**

**Several schools.** If you have children at more than one school, each school creates its own account for you. The mobile app can hold these accounts and let you switch between them, but they stay separate and we never combine them.

### 3.2 School records

**Responsible:** your school.

What is recorded depends on the school. Studafy is built to hold:

- **Students:** names (including a preferred name), admission number, date of birth and nationality (both optional), admission date, enrolment status, grade, section and classes, and leaving date.
- **Parents and guardians:** name, contact details, and the link to each child with the relationship and household. Only school staff create these links, by choosing both people; Studafy never links people automatically.
- **Learning:** attendance (including lateness, reasons and corrections), assignments, quizzes and exams set by teachers, submitted work and attachments, marks and changes to them with reasons, results, certificates, progress reports and teacher comments.
- **Behaviour:** discipline incidents, which can include the type (for example bullying or a safety concern), a description, and actions such as suspension or a counselling referral.
- **Staff:** employee number, employment status and dates, and teacher evaluations (ratings, comments and scores), visible to the teacher once shared with them.
- **Custom records and lists:** each school can design its own record forms for students, parents, staff and classes, and its own lists, which can hold almost any information, including documents such as certificates or medical forms.
- **Imports:** when a school imports students from a spreadsheet, we keep the original file for a limited time (section 10).

**Sensitive fields.** A school can mark any custom field as sensitive. Sensitive values are encrypted by the application before storage, shown masked (for example `•••• 1234`), and can be revealed only by the school's administrators and principal (for staff records, administrators only). Every reveal, and every download of a sensitive document, is recorded in the audit log, without the value itself.

**Health and similar information.** A school may record information it needs about health, special educational needs or similar matters ("special categories" of data). The school decides whether it needs it and is responsible for the lawful basis and any condition the law requires. We process it only for the school, and recommend that schools mark such fields as sensitive.

**Government identification.** Studafy never asks for a national ID, passport or similar number. A school may record one in a custom field only where the law requires or allows it, and Studafy suggests marking such a field as sensitive. **Studafy never uses government identification, or any name, email address or phone number, to match, merge or link people.**

### 3.3 Messages between parents and teachers

**Responsible:** your school.

Parents and teachers can message each other about a child the teacher teaches. Students cannot send or receive messages. We store each message's sender, text (up to 2,000 characters), time and any images (up to four).

Only the two people in a conversation can read it in the app; school leaders cannot, unless a participant reports a message. A report gives the school's administrators and principal a copy of the reported message and up to 20 earlier messages, with the senders' names. The school decides what to do and keeps reports as safeguarding records. Messages cannot be edited or deleted once sent. Blocking someone stops new messages; earlier ones remain visible.

### 3.4 Notifications and email

**Responsible:** your school for content about school matters; Studafy for delivering it.

Studafy sends in-app notifications, mobile push notifications and emails, for example about marks, attendance alerts, announcements, fee notices and account changes. **These can contain personal data,** such as a student's name, an assignment title and mark, a fee amount and due date, or, in the in-app notice of a new message, the sender's name and the first 140 characters of the message. Push notifications go through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and emails through Resend (section 8). We keep a copy of each notification and a record of each email's delivery.

You can turn push notifications off in your device's settings, and adjust some notification preferences in the app.

### 3.5 Fees and finance

**Responsible:** your school.

If your school uses Studafy for fees, it holds the school's invoices, payments, receipts, fee schedules, discounts (sibling discounts use dates of birth to order siblings), scholarships, refunds, families, expenses (including suppliers' names) and tax settings.

If your school offers online payment, you pay on a secure page hosted by a payment provider. We send it the payer's name and email and references to the invoice and student, and receive the result. We never receive or store full card numbers.

### 3.6 Security, logs and error reports

**Responsible:** Studafy. The audit log is also part of the school's records, which its administrators can review.

To keep Studafy secure, find faults and prevent misuse, we record:

- **an audit log** of actions in Studafy: who did what, to which record and when, the values before and after a change, and the IP address and user agent of the person acting. Searches, including the search text, are recorded; message text is not;
- **security events**, such as blocked sign-in attempts, with IP address and user agent;
- **rate-limit counters**, which use IP addresses, to stop excessive requests;
- **application logs** of requests to our servers, including the address requested (which can contain search terms or identifiers), user and school identifiers, browser or app details and, in some cases, the IP address;
- **error reports** to Sentry, with your user identifier and technical details, after automatic filters remove email addresses, tokens and phone numbers where they detect them. Sentry may also receive the sending device's IP address;
- **mobile crash reports** to Firebase Crashlytics, with crash details, device model, operating system, app installation identifiers and your user identifier.

**IP addresses are stored as they are, not in hashed form,** in the audit log, security events, session records and rate-limit counters.

### 3.7 School applications and sales inquiries

**Responsible:** Studafy.

**Applications.** A person applying for their school to use Studafy signs in with Google or Microsoft and gives their name, job title, phone number and email, the school's name, email, country, city, address, website and approximate number of students, a message, and one document showing they represent the school. The application is emailed to our onboarding inbox, and our team reviews it and records the decision and notes. A cookie keeps the applicant signed in to the application for two hours.

**Sales inquiries.** When you use the "Talk to us" form, we keep your name, email, phone number, school name, job title, country, number of students, message and the page you sent it from, and our team may add notes. The inquiry is emailed to our sales inbox. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile (section 15), which receives your IP address; we do not store your IP address with the inquiry.

### 3.8 Payments to Studafy

**Responsible:** Studafy.

- **School licences.** Studafy is free for schools. A school may buy optional seat licences: we send a quote and invoice, the school pays by bank transfer and may upload its receipt, and we record the payment. This involves the administrators' names and contact details and the details on the receipt.
- **The AI add-on.** A student can buy the AI study assistant in the mobile app through Google Play or the App Store. Apple and Google take the payment and handle refunds; we never receive card details. The store sends us a purchase record, which we verify and link to the student's account, using identifiers for the student and school, so the add-on is switched on for the right person. We keep records of purchases, renewals and refund requests; our staff decide refund requests made to us.

### 3.9 Our website

**Responsible:** Studafy.

Our website and web app use no analytics or advertising tools. Our servers may record technical details of requests, such as IP address and browser details, as part of normal logs. Section 15 covers cookies and browser storage.

## 5. The AI study assistant

### 5.1 What it is

The AI study assistant helps students study from their class materials: it answers questions about lessons ("Ask AI"), summarises materials, explains key concepts, and creates quizzes, flashcards and practice exams. Only students can use it, and only for themselves. It does not mark work and makes no decision about any student.

A student can get it **through the school,** on a licence seat the school assigns, in which case the AI processing is School Data and the school is responsible, or **by buying the add-on themselves** in the mobile app, in which case Studafy is the controller of the purchase, the entitlement and the AI processing for that add-on. Either way, the student must agree to the data sharing below first.

### 5.2 What is sent to our AI provider

The assistant runs on a model provided by **Together AI** (Together Computer, Inc.) in the **United States**. We send Together AI:

- **the student's questions** and, in Ask AI, up to six earlier messages from the conversation; and
- **the text of the class materials** used (and a material's title, for summaries). Materials are written by teachers and others and **can contain names or other personal details.**

We do **not** send the student's name, email address, age, date of birth, country, marks, attendance or any user identifier.

**Photos.** A student can photograph a page to turn it into text. The text is read from the photo on our own servers; the photo is not stored and is never sent to Together AI. Only the extracted text is sent, if the student uses it.

**Filters, not redaction.** An automatic filter blocks questions that appear to contain a phone number, email address, card number or identity number, and a blocked question is not sent. Answers also pass a safety filter. These filters catch some personal details but are not a complete redaction: names and other details in questions or materials can still be sent. Please do not type personal details about yourself or others into the assistant.

### 5.3 Consent

Before first use, each student is shown a consent screen naming Together AI and the kinds of data sent. Nothing is sent until the student taps "Allow". A student can withdraw at any time in the app (AI tab, privacy icon, "AI data sharing"). After withdrawal nothing more is sent; data already sent cannot be recalled, and stored conversations remain until their 90-day period ends. If the provider or the kinds of data change, every student is asked again.

**Students under 16 should use or buy the AI study assistant only with a parent's or guardian's permission.** The app does not know a student's age and does not check this, so we ask families to follow this rule. Parents can also use the stores' own purchase controls.

### 5.4 What we keep

- **Ask AI conversations:** 90 days, then deleted automatically.
- **Summaries:** held temporarily for 24 hours to avoid generating them twice.
- **Quizzes, flashcards and practice exams** a student creates, with their answers and review schedule: kept with the student's account.
- **Consent records** (when given and withdrawn, and which version) and **monthly usage counts.**
- **Safety records.** When the filter blocks something, we keep a fingerprint (hash) of the text, not the text. If a student reports an AI answer, a copy goes to the school's administrators, who review reports. If the filter blocks the most serious content, involving the abuse of children, an urgent report with the blocked text is filed automatically for the school's administrators. Where a school cannot act, Studafy may report to the authorities where the law requires or allows.

### 5.5 Accuracy and training

AI answers can be wrong or incomplete. Students should check them against their materials and ask their teachers. **Teachers decide all marks.**

We do not use School Data or students' conversations to train AI models, or for advertising.

## 11. Deleting your account

### 11.1 How to delete

Anyone with an account, including a student, can ask for it to be deleted:

- **in the app:** in your account settings at app.studafy.com or in the mobile app. Member-ID accounts confirm with their member ID and password;
- **without signing in:** at [/legal/delete-account](/legal/delete-account). After a short check, we email you a single-use link, valid for one hour, which covers **every Studafy account with that email address, at every school**. For your privacy, the page never says whether an account exists.

You can also ask your school to remove your account.

### 11.2 What happens next

1. **Straight away,** you are signed out on all devices.
2. **For 14 days,** you can change your mind. If you sign in during this time, you can only cancel the deletion or sign out.
3. **After 14 days,** your account is closed: your roles, Google or Microsoft sign-in connection, parent–child links, sessions and devices are removed, and we email you a confirmation. Erasure of your personal data is then completed **within 30 days.**

Erasure removes your name, email address and similar contact details from your account and profile, and the answers in your school's custom record forms and lists that relate to you, with their documents.

## 14. How we protect data

Our measures, suited to the kinds of data Studafy holds, including children's data, include:

- **encryption in transit** for connections to Studafy and between our servers and the database;
- **encryption of sensitive fields:** fields a school marks as sensitive are encrypted by the application (AES-256) before storage, masked on screen, and revealed only by authorised school leaders, with every reveal recorded;
- **access by role:** each person sees only what their role needs, and each school's data is kept separate from other schools';
- **protected secrets:** passwords are stored only as Argon2id hashes, and sign-in tokens, invitation links and setup codes only as hashes. On phones, sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage and excluded from Android backups;
- **private files,** opened through links that expire within minutes (sensitive documents within one minute);
- **limits on misuse:** requests are rate-limited, and member-ID sign-in locks for 15 minutes after five failed attempts;
- **virus scanning of uploaded files,** such as class materials, homework, message images, record documents and receipts, on our own servers before anyone else can open them, with infected files held back. Files uploaded before we extended scanning to them in November 2026 may not have been scanned;
- **audit records** of significant actions, including reveals of sensitive data and searches.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects School Data, we tell the school without undue delay, as the [School Agreement](/school-agreement) requires, so it can meet its obligations. If a breach affects data we are responsible for, we notify the Data Protection Commission and, where the law requires, the people affected.
