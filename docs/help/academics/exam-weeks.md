# Plan exam weeks

Principals and school admins plan exam weeks. Students, parents and teachers
see an exam period in the app only after you publish it.

## Open exam weeks

From your `Home` page, click `Exam weeks →`. Pick the **Academic year**.

## Set up the rooms

Under **Rooms**, click `Add room` for each room you hold exams in. Fill in the
**Exam capacity**: how many students sit an exam there with spacing. Leave it
empty to use the room's everyday capacity. A room with neither seats nobody.

## Add an exam period

Click `Add exam period`. Give it a **Name** (for example "Term 1 finals"), a
**Kind**, the **First day** and **Last day**, and tick the **Grades it covers**.
It starts as a draft.

## Build the exam timetable

Click `Open` on the period, then `Add exam` for each exam:

- the **Grade**, **Subject** and the **Sections sitting it**;
- the **Date**, **Starts** and **Ends** times, in school time;
- the **Rooms**, ticked in the order seats are given;
- optionally the **Gradebook assessment** the exam is, such as "Final exam".

Students exempt from a subject, and students who do not take an optional
subject, are left out of its exam.

## Seat the students

Click `Seating` on an exam and choose how to seat students:

- **By section**: section by section, each in name order.
- **Alphabetically**: everyone in name order.
- **Mixed sections**: one student from each section in turn, so neighbours are
  from different sections.

Click `Generate seating`. With "Keep the seats already given" ticked, students
who already have a seat keep it, so you can regenerate after a change without
moving everyone. Two exams in the same hall at the same time never share a
seat. To change one seat, click `Move`: if a student of the same exam sits
there, the two swap.

## Assign invigilators

Click `Invigilators` on an exam and tick who watches each room.

## Fix clashes

**Clashes** lists problems first, then warnings: a teacher in two rooms at
once, students with two exams at once, a room with more students than seats,
students without a seat, a room nobody watches, or an exam on a school holiday.
Fix each one by changing the time, the rooms, the invigilators or the seating.

## Publish and print

Click `Publish`. Students and parents now see the student's exams with room
and seat in the app, and teachers their invigilation duties. Click `Unpublish`
to hide the timetable again.

Click `Print seating lists` for each exam's students in name order with room
and seat, or `Print door sheets` for each room's seats for one sitting, to put
on the door.
