# Take attendance

Registers are taken in the Studafy mobile app, by teachers and by the
attendance office. On the web, school admins and principals can see every
register, and principals can correct records.

## Take attendance as a teacher

1. In the mobile app, on your `Home` tab, tap `Take attendance`.
2. If you teach more than one class, choose it under **Which class?**. Today's
   register for that class opens.
3. Everyone starts as present. Change anyone who isn't to `Absent`, `Late` or
   `Excused`. A late student needs the minutes late (1 or more).
4. Tap `Submit register`, then `Submit` to confirm.

Once submitted, the register is read-only for you. To change a record, ask the
attendance office.

## Take a register as the attendance office

1. In the mobile app, open the `Registers` tab. Pick the term and the register
   date if they are not today's.
2. Tap a class. Each class shows `Not started`, `In progress` or `Submitted`.
3. Use `Mark all present` if you need to reset everyone, then change anyone who
   is absent, late or excused.
4. Tap `Submit register`, then `Submit` to confirm.

## Check registers on the web

In the sidebar, open `Attendance` and pick the **Date**. Each class shows its
register as `Not started`, `In progress` or `Submitted`. Click
`View register` to see who was marked what.

## Correct a record

After a register is submitted, a change needs a correction with a reason. The
reason is kept in the attendance history.

- **Attendance office (mobile):** tap `Correct` next to the student.
- **Principal (web):** open the register, click `Correct` next to the student,
  pick the **Corrected status**, enter the **Reason**, and click
  `Submit correction`.

Corrections are allowed within the correction window: 48 hours by default,
set by the school admin under School settings → **Attendance alerts** →
**Correction window (hours)**. After it closes, only a principal can correct a
record.
