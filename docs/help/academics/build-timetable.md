# Build the timetable

Principals and school admins build the timetable. Everyone else sees it
read-only. For every option on the screen, see [Timetable](/help/timetable).

## Before you start

Add the term's classes first, each with its usual teacher (principal portal →
`Classes →`). The timetable places those classes into the week.

## Open the timetable

- **School admin:** from your `Home` page, click `Build timetable →`.
- **Principal:** from your `Home` page, click `Timetable →`.

Pick the **Academic year** and **Term**. The current ones are selected for you.

## Start a draft

Click `Create timetable` if the term has none yet, or `Edit timetable` to
change the live one. You always work on a draft: teachers and students keep
seeing the live timetable until you publish.

## Set the school week

Click `School week`, tick the **Teaching days**, set the **Periods per day**,
and click `Save school week`. The grid shows those days as columns and those
periods as rows.

## Add lessons

- Hover a cell, click `+`, choose the **Class** (its usual teacher is filled
  in), and click `Add lesson`.
- Or drag a class from the `Classes` palette onto a cell.
- Or click `Import CSV` to add many lessons from a spreadsheet.

Click a lesson to move it, change its teacher, or remove it.

## Fix clashes

If a teacher is already booked at that day and period, Studafy stops the
lesson and outlines the one it clashes with in red. Move one of the two
lessons, or give it another teacher.

## Publish

Click `Publish`. The draft becomes the live timetable straight away. To throw
your changes away instead, click `Discard draft`. If you leave and come back,
`Continue editing` reopens your draft.
