# Subscription management

The school's own Studafy subscription — plan, seats, payment method, and
cancellation — lives in Billing. This is distinct from `Finance`, which bills
families for tuition.

Open Billing from the portal. The page has two cards: **Plan and seats** and
**Payment and invoices**.

## Subscription states

A status pill on the Plan card tells you where the subscription stands:

| State          | Meaning                                                                                                                              |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `Trialing`     | Free trial period.                                                                                                                   |
| `Active`       | All good.                                                                                                                            |
| `Past due`     | The last payment failed. Update your payment method before the grace period starts.                                                  |
| `Grace period` | Payment failed and you are in the grace window. Update your payment method before it ends, or the subscription closes automatically. |
| `Canceled`     | Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current period.                                                                         |
| `Expired`      | Ended. Choose a plan to restore access.                                                                                              |
| `Closed`       | Ended. Choose a plan to restore access.                                                                                              |
| `Paused`       | Temporarily paused.                                                                                                                  |

`Past due` and `Grace period` also show a banner at the top of the page with a
`Update payment method` button. An ended subscription shows a `Choose a plan`
banner instead.

## Seats

The Plan card shows seats used out of the plan's cap, for example "12/20",
with a meter. Seats are who can hold an account in the school. Stay under the
cap — if you are at the limit, add seats by changing to a larger plan.

## Changing plan

1. `Change plan` on the Plan card.
2. Pick a plan. Options show `Display name — price/mo` or `/yr`, with
   `(current plan)` marked.
3. `Continue to checkout`. This starts a checkout session for the new plan's
   seat-based price; the current plan stays active until checkout completes.

## Managing the payment method

`Payment and invoices` → `Manage payment method` opens the payment provider's
own portal where you update the card, tax details, and past receipts. Studafy
does not store card details.

## Cancelling

1. `Cancel subscription` on the Plan card.

Cancellation is scheduled for the **end of the current billing period**, not
immediate. Access and billing continue until then. You can undo it any time
before the end with `Keep subscription`. An optional **Reason** field helps us
improve; it is not required.

A note like "Cancellation scheduled — access ends {date}." appears on the Plan
card once cancellation is scheduled.

## Invoice history

Billing → `View invoice history` opens the provider's invoices, most recent
first: date, period, status, amount due/paid, and `View`/`PDF` document links.
This is the school's own billing history — tuition invoices to families are
under Finance → Invoices.

## AI add-on

The optional per-student AI add-on (Ask AI, flashcards, and study-material
summaries) is purchased separately from the school plan. Parents can "unlock"
it per child from the mobile app; the purchase screen is also reachable from
the school's own account (`/account/ai`).
